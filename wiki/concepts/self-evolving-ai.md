# Self-Evolving AI

**Self-evolving AI** refers to systems that participate in their own training and improvement loop. [[MiniMax]]'s M2.7 demonstrated this by building agent harnesses, running RL experiments, and iterating on its own scaffold for 100+ autonomous rounds.

## Key Ideas

- Model assists in training loop but researchers still set goals and make key decisions
- M2.7 achieved 30% performance improvement through autonomous scaffold iteration
- Future direction: full autonomy coordinating data construction, training, evaluation without human involvement

## How It Connects

- [[Reinforcement Learning]] - mechanism for self-improvement
- [[Agentic AI]] - self-evolution requires agentic capabilities
- [[MiniMax]] - primary proponent

## Data gaps

- No external reproducibility of self-evolution claims
- Safety implications of recursive self-improvement not addressed
