# Medusa

A [[20260608-120000-speculative-decoding-概念]] method that adds several **extra LM heads** on top of the target model, each predicting a token at a different future step from the same hidden states. Requires Medusa fine-tuned weights. In vLLM it is the `medusa` method (`MedusaProposer`, ~78 lines, one of the simplest). Rated medium benefit on latency and throughput. Conceptually related to [[20260608-121100-mtp-multi-token-prediction-概念]] (both add heads to the target) but Medusa needs separate fine-tuning.
