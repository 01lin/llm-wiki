# DFlash (DeepSeek Flash MTP)

**DFlash** is a [[20260608-120000-speculative-decoding-概念]] method exposed in vLLM as `dflash` (`DflashProposer`), requiring DeepSeek MTP weights and rated high benefit on both latency and throughput. It is a DeepSeek-flavored variant of [[20260608-121100-mtp-multi-token-prediction-概念]].

Open questions captured in the raw notes:
- **No open-source DFlash training work exists for large MoE** - possibly limited by training resources or unresolved technical issues ([[20260608-122500-投机优化方向-dflash-训练-想法-来源]]).
- A SGLang/[[20260608-123100-specforge-实体]] bug ([[20260608-123000-specforge-dflash-low-acceptance-bug-455-来源]], issue #455) reports **trained DFlash with extremely low acceptance rate (~1.0-1.3)** despite normal training convergence - a cautionary signal that the *training recipe* (not inference) is the blocker. ZLab hasn't released official training details; alignment of chat template, loss (CE vs KL), and `mask_token_id`/`target_layer_ids` matters.

## Data gaps
- Root cause of the SpecForge training gap never definitively isolated; waits on ZLab's official release.
- DFlash vs standard DeepSeek MTP performance delta is undocumented here.
- No large-MoE DFlash data point (the open-source gap noted in [[20260608-122500-投机优化方向-dflash-训练-想法-来源]]).
