# PPO

**Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO)** was the standard RL algorithm for LLM training (used in RLHF). Requires separate reward and critic models, making it resource-intensive. Being superseded by [[20260412-194111-grpo-概念|GRPO]] which eliminates these requirements.
