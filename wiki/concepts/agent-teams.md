# Agent Teams

**Agent Teams** is a multi-agent collaboration paradigm requiring role boundaries, adversarial reasoning, protocol adherence, and behavioral differentiation. [[MiniMax]] argues these must be internalized as native model capabilities, not achieved through prompting alone.

## Key Ideas

- Models must stably anchor role identity and proactively challenge teammates' blind spots
- MiniMax uses Agent Teams internally for product prototype development
