# LMSYS

**LMSYS** (Large Model Systems Organization) is a research organization known for Chatbot Arena (LLM evaluation platform), [[SGLang]] (serving framework), and inference optimization research including [[lmsys-2026-hisparse|HiSparse]] and HiCache.
