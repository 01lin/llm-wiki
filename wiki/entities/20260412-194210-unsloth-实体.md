# Unsloth

**Unsloth** is an open-source framework for efficient LLM fine-tuning and [[20260412-194030-reinforcement-learning-概念|Reinforcement Learning]] training. Integrates with [[20260412-194210-nemo-gym-实体|NeMo Gym]] for consuming RL environment rollouts. Founded by Daniel and Michael Han.
