# vLLM-Ascend

**vLLM-Ascend** (`vllm-project/vllm-ascend`) is the Huawei **Ascend NPU** adaptation of [[20260412-194210-vllm-实体]]. While upstream vLLM owns the general serving framework and speculative-decoding algorithms, vLLM-Ascend lands the NPU-specific implementation.

## What it provides
- **Spec-decode on NPU**: `AscendEagleProposer` (EAGLE/EAGLE-3/MTP - MTP reuses the EAGLE path), NPU Triton rejection sampling kernels, worker patches (`patch_deepseek_mtp`, `patch_rejection_sampler`). See [[20260608-121100-mtp-multi-token-prediction-概念]].
- **Graph mode**: ACL Graph (the CUDA-Graph equivalent), FULL_DECODE_ONLY, fullgraph capture-size constraints.
- **MoE / comm**: flashcomm v1, MoE dispatch (`MoeDistributeDispatchV4`), EP.
- **GDN/Mamba** path for Qwen3.5/Qwen3-Next (custom `_forward_core` due to incomplete `torch_npu` support).
- Quantization fast paths (MTP decode fast path requires `w8a8_dynamic`).

## Hardware
Primary: Ascend **910B3 / A2** (Atlas 800T A2). 910C emerging but no dedicated validation line yet. Key operator limits: `npu_fused_infer_attention_score` caps MTP at 15.

## Known pain points
mtp+graph startup failures, PD-disaggregation instability at high concurrency, A2 MoE/EP dispatch limits, HCCL/FFTS timeouts. See [[20260608-120900-qwen3-5-mtp-ascend-适配分析-来源]].

Closely tied to [[20260412-194210-qwen-实体]], [[20260412-194210-glm-实体]], [[20260412-194210-deepseek-实体]].
