# DeepGEMM

**DeepGEMM** (`deepseek-ai/DeepGEMM`) is [[20260412-194210-deepseek-实体]]'s open-source GEMM/kernel library: FP8/FP4 GEMM, MoE GEMM layouts (DeepEPv2), GEMM heuristics, JIT compilation. The 26/04 release (PR #304) added **Mega MoE** (a fused mega-kernel overlapping NVLink comm with tensor-core compute, FP8xFP4, EP<=8) and an **FP4 Indexer** with larger MTP support. See [[20260608-122600-deepgemm-mega-moe-fp4-indexer-release-来源]]. Relates to [[20260412-194111-quantization-概念]].
